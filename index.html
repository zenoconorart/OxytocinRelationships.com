FAQ

Oxytocin without the mythology.

Is oxytocin really the “love hormone”?
It participates in social, reproductive and stress-related biology, but human behavioural effects depend heavily on context. The nickname is useful shorthand, not a complete explanation.
Has intranasal oxytocin been studied in couples?
Yes. Controlled studies have examined couple conflict, romantic touch, jealousy and partner support. These studies do not establish a general relationship treatment.
Does this site recommend taking oxytocin?
No. This is an editorial science site and does not provide dosing or self-administration instructions.
