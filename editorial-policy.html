EDITORIAL POLICY

How we handle the science.

The editorial site is designed to remain useful even for readers who never visit the affiliated store.

Primary sources first

Where practical, summaries link directly to PubMed or another primary publication record.

Different evidence types stay separate

Natural hormone correlations do not establish what administered oxytocin will do. We distinguish endogenous hormone research from administration experiments.

Limitations belong in the article

Context, sample size, sex-specific findings and the gap between laboratory outcomes and real-world relationship outcomes are not hidden as fine print.

No dosing or personal-use instructions

This site does not tell readers how to administer oxytocin or how much to take.

Commercial relationship

Oxytocin Relationships is commercially affiliated with OxytoZen Lab. Store links are promotional referrals, not independent endorsements.
